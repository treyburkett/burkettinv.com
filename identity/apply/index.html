Decks and PDF

Skeleton rules. Not templates.

  • Cover: black field, stacked or horizontal wordmark, one title, no framed B.
  • Interior: hairline grids, label-above-value specs, gold for section ignition only.
  • PDF export: radius 0, no soft shadows, no decorative card stacks.
  • Body type stays Geist / system. Avoid display serifs and cream paper fills.

Social stills

Architecture. Material. Chrome.

  • Still: wordmark or one architecture plate. No meme overlays.
  • Avatar: favicon mark only if a square crop is required. Prefer wordmark on banners.
  • Captions follow VOICE.md. Expertise language. No ownership claims.
  • Do not depend on trend audio, emoji, or soft glass stickers.
