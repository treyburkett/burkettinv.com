Clearspace

Give the mark room to read.

Minimum

Keep empty space around the lockup at least equal to the height of the capital B. No competing logos, icons, or copy inside that zone.

Framed B

The white B on black exists for favicon and app icon only. Do not enlarge it into a hero mark, social avatar monument, or deck cover badge.

Favicon

Tab chrome. Not marketing.

Files

Site icons load from /brand/mark.svg. If a tab still shows a stale glyph, close and reopen it. Chrome caches favicons by domain. Apple touch icon may use a PNG export of the same mark; do not invent a second logo.

Rules

Sharp. Locked. Readable.

Use
  • Shipped SVG lockups from /brand only.
  • Name-only (Burkett.) for product UI, intranet, and owner OS chrome.
  • Full lockup (Burkett. Investments) for public marketing and legal entity surfaces.
  • Dark file on black or steel. Light file on white.
  • Stacked lockup for email signatures.
  • Clearspace: at least the height of the B on all sides.
Ban
  • Framed B as a marketing monument.
  • Recoloring the wordmark outside shipped files.
  • Stretching, outlining, or dropping shadow on the mark.
  • Favicon B as a substitute for the wordmark on page chrome.
  • Using Investments lockup on owner intranet / product chrome by default.
