Who We Are

Decades of operating edge.
One standard of rigor.

Burkett Investments, LLC is a real estate consulting and investment management firm founded by two seasoned professionals with deep roots across the industry.

We provide institutional-grade real estate consulting to clients navigating complex markets, investment decisions, and portfolio strategy across the United States.

Our approach combines decades of hands-on operational experience with a client-first philosophy, delivering thoughtful, data-driven guidance at every stage of the investment lifecycle.

Principal credentials across the firm

Kyle Burkett holds an MBA from Alliance Manchester Business School and the CAPS designation, with senior leadership experience at Nicol Investment Company, Greystar, and Pegasus Residential.

Trey Burkett is a Project Management Professional (PMP) and University of Minnesota graduate with a background in finance, operations, and strategic project management.

Together, they bring underwriting discipline, operational depth, and the back-office infrastructure to execute at scale.
