Burkett Investments, LLC is a Nashville-based real estate consulting and investment management firm founded by two seasoned professionals with deep roots in the industry.

We specialize in providing institutional-grade real estate consulting services to clients navigating complex markets, investment decisions, and portfolio strategy across the United States.

Our approach combines decades of hands-on operational experience with a client-first philosophy — delivering thoughtful, data-driven guidance at every stage of the investment lifecycle.

Our Leadership

The Faces Behind
Burkett Investments

Kyle Burkett

Co-Founder

With over 20 years of experience in the real estate industry, Kyle brings a wealth of expertise spanning multifamily, senior housing, self-storage, retail, and short-term rental investments across the country. He holds an MBA from Alliance Manchester Business School and the CAPS designation, and has served as a senior leader at Nicol Investment Company, Greystar, and Pegasus Residential. A board member of The Gulch Nashville, Kyle is deeply connected to the national real estate community. He also advises property owners and managers on specialized insurance and risk management products that generate meaningful revenue back to their operations — a unique value-add that sets Burkett Investments apart.

Trey Burkett

Co-Founder

Trey is a Project Management Professional (PMP) and University of Minnesota graduate with a background in finance, operations, and strategic project management. He brings a disciplined, analytical approach to real estate investing, combining rigorous process management with a sharp eye for market opportunity. Beyond deal strategy, Trey oversees the firm's back office operations and technology infrastructure — ensuring Burkett Investments runs efficiently, securely, and at scale.
