Principles

How we decide what to recommend.

01

Underwrite the downside first

We model the worst case before we model the best one. If a deal only works when everything goes right, we do not recommend it.

02

Buy durable cash flow

We favor assets with resilient, needs-based demand over stories that only work while a market stays hot.

03

Advise through the full stack

Underwriting, operations, and stewardship should stay connected. Advice that dies at handoff is not advice clients can use.

04

Think in long holds

We structure recommendations and expectations around patience, not a forced exit on someone else's timeline.
