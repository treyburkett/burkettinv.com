Why we exist.

Burkett Investments is founder-led. You work directly with Kyle and Trey, not a rotating cast of associates handing off deliverables. That means faster decisions, accountable advice, and partners who stay in the work long after the deal closes.

We pair decades of operational leadership at scale with the discipline and back-office infrastructure to advise like an institution, without the bureaucracy of a large shop.

Burkett Investments operates with a national footprint. We are small enough that every decision runs through the founders, and disciplined enough that clients get the underwriting rigor and reporting they would expect from a much larger institution.

The principals.

Co-Founder

Kyle Burkett

With over 20 years of experience in real estate, Kyle brings expertise spanning multifamily, senior housing, self-storage, retail, and short-term rental investments across the country. He holds an MBA from Alliance Manchester Business School and the CAPS designation, and has served as a senior leader at Nicol Investment Company, Greystar, and Pegasus Residential. A board member of The Gulch Nashville, Kyle also advises owners and managers on specialized insurance and risk-management products that generate meaningful revenue back to their operations, a value-add unique to Burkett.

Kyle@burkettinv.com

Co-Founder

Trey Burkett

Trey is a Project Management Professional (PMP) and University of Minnesota graduate with a background in finance, operations, and strategic project management. He brings a disciplined, analytical approach to investing, combining rigorous process management with a sharp eye for market opportunity. Beyond deal strategy, Trey oversees the firm's back-office operations and technology infrastructure, ensuring Burkett runs efficiently, securely, and at scale.

Trey@burkettinv.com
