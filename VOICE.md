## Partner / deck disclaimer

Canonical string (copy chip on Identity Apply; footer on partner one-pager and deck skeleton):

## Client work vs firm capital

We advise and underwrite **for clients**. Do not present client assets, deals, or classes as Burkett-owned holdings. "Building toward ownership" is internal aspiration only until true on public/partner surfaces.

## Engagement taxonomy

Three lanes. Label the engagement. Do not collapse them.

### Say

- **Advisory:** underwriting, acquisition strategy, and stewardship **for clients**
- **Investment management (IM):** managing or advising on **client** capital and mandates
- **Future capital path:** "building toward firm capital" only as aspiration, never as present ownership or AUM

### Never

- Present advisory or IM work as firm-owned holdings or firm AUM
- Use "our portfolio" / "assets we own" under any engagement label
- Market a live owner portal or capital deployment as current fact until true
